INVICTUS User Guide

Everything you need to use the app confidently.
Welcome to INVICTUS. This guide explains how to record daily attendance, mark individual lectures, manage results, review progress, and use the current Settings controls. INVICTUS keeps attendance, lecture tracking, and results isolated by semester while bringing the relevant views together in the main tabs.

🚀 1. Getting Started

After signing in, complete the information INVICTUS requests, especially your College Joining Date. The four main tabs are:

AttendanceRecord college-day attendance, review Attendance History, and use the calendar.
SemesterUse the Daily Timetable, Lecture Breakdown, submitted results, and Assignment Collection for the active semester.
DashboardReview weekly, monthly, and semester-level lecture and attendance reports.
SettingsManage your account, semester preference, app options, data, display, security, appearance, and help.

After the first successful login, INVICTUS opens the User Guide in a required first-login flow. Scroll through the guide and use the Close button at the bottom. When you open the guide normally later, use the top-right × button.

📅 2. College Joining Date

Your College Joining Date defines the earliest date from which INVICTUS treats attendance as eligible for your account. The selectable range begins on 08 August 2026 and ends on the current date.

When no joining date is saved, INVICTUS asks you to choose one before starting attendance-related actions that require it.

To change an existing date, open Settings → Account → Edit Joined date. The current date is prefilled. Select the corrected date and tap Save Changes.

Changing the joining date changes the attendance-eligibility cutoff used by INVICTUS. Existing attendance records are not deleted or rewritten. Dates before the new joining date are excluded from the applicable attendance tracking and calculations.

📝 3. Submit Your Attendance

Open Attendance → Submit Your Attendance to record the status of the current college day. Choose the available status — Present, Absent, Holiday, or Blank — and provide the required reason or details.

For a normal college day, choose Present → Regular College Day. The normal Present submission window is 08:30 AM–09:30 AM. A Present submission made after that window is accepted as a Late Submission. The existing status-specific validation still applies.

Before opening today's submission form, INVICTUS checks for earlier eligible dates that have no attendance record. The check is performed in two separate gates, in this order:

Gate 1 — Attendance Needs ResolutionChecks the other affected semesters. Each listed semester opens its own missed-date resolution flow. Resolve those dates and return to the cross-semester checker.
Gate 2 — Attendance Not MarkedAfter the cross-semester check, checks unresolved dates in the semester containing today's date. The missed-date list supports individual actions and Mark Date Range.

Both gates include Continue to Today's Attendance Anyway when they are acting as the relevant submission gate. At Gate 1, Continue bypasses only the cross-semester gate and INVICTUS still checks Gate 2. At Gate 2, Continue bypasses the current-semester gate and opens today's attendance form.

Continue is a temporary bypass for the current submission attempt only. It does not mark, delete, or change any unresolved historical dates. Those dates remain available to resolve later. Closing a gate cancels the bypass attempt; closing the Submit Attendance form resets the bypasses.

The date being submitted determines the target semester for today's attendance. The Attendance tab displays a unified, read-only view assembled from the six isolated semester attendance stores; attendance submissions and edits remain within the applicable semester's store.

🗓️ 4. Attendance History & Calendar

Use Attendance History to review saved day-level attendance. The history can be filtered by Status and Month. Entries can show their recorded reason, submission time, and a Late Submission or Edited marker where applicable.

The Calendar displays attendance dates across the available range from August 2026 through July 2029. Swipe between months or use the calendar month picker to jump to another available month.

The calendar summary shows three semester-scoped counts:

HolidaysHoliday dates within the selected semester's configured range.
BlankDay-level Blank attendance records within the selected semester.
WeekendsElapsed Saturdays and Sundays that are non-working days; dates recorded Present or Absent as working-day exceptions are excluded.

Protected college records are identified as automatic in Attendance History and cannot be changed through the normal attendance controls.

📚 5. Daily Timetable

Open Semester → Daily Timetable to view the timetable for the active semester and selected date. Swipe left or right to move between days, or use the calendar button to choose a date.

On an eligible day with an attendance record, tap a lecture's three-dot action to choose its lecture status:

AttendedRecords the lecture as attended. If an Adjustment applies, the linked adjustment rules are used.
AbsentRecords that you were absent from the lecture.
Teacher AbsentRecords that the teacher was absent for the scheduled lecture.
No ClassesRecords that the scheduled lecture did not take place.

For today's lectures, the attendance action becomes available when the lecture's scheduled start time is reached. A date with no day-level attendance record does not expose the normal scheduled lecture actions.

🕐 6. Schedule Exception

Use Schedule Exception when a particular day's timetable differs from its normal schedule. On an eligible Present → Regular College Day, tap the clock beside the date to open the day's schedule exception controls.

Set ReplacementChanges which lecture occupies the selected timetable slot.
Set AdjustmentKeeps the original scheduled slot for tracking but sends the attendance credit to the selected adjustment subject when you mark attendance.

Choose the lecture slot, select the required option, and confirm the change. A Replacement is confirmed before it is applied. The updated Schedule Exception view remains available so you can make another change if required.

🔄 7. Adjustment Period

Use an Adjustment when you attended a different subject during a scheduled lecture period.

Go to Daily Timetable → clock → lecture → Set Adjustment. Choose a category — Subjects, Lab, or Others — then select the class and apply it.

When the adjusted lecture is marked, the original scheduled slot remains tracked as Teacher Absent, while the selected adjustment subject receives the outcome you chose. The linked outcomes are used by the lecture analytics.

When editing an existing Adjustment, choosing the original scheduled subject removes that Adjustment.

↩️ 8. Set to Default

Use Set to Default in Schedule Exception to remove a timetable change for the selected date. If both types of change exist, choose what to restore:

AdjustmentRemove only the Adjustment.
ReplacementRemove only the Replacement.
BothRemove both changes for that date.

Confirm with Yes. Choosing No leaves the existing change untouched.

📌 Set to Default changes the selected timetable exception only. It is not the same as Reset All App Data.

🔎 9. Attendance & Lecture Review

Check Unmarked Lectures finds eligible lectures on previous dates that still need a mark. It scans previous days, not today's unfinished lectures, and follows the app's Present + Regular College Day eligibility rules.

Lecture Breakdown summarizes lecture outcomes for the active semester. Lecture Wise Percentage shows subject-level lecture performance and the available outcome distribution.

The lecture distribution can include:

Attended / Adjustment AttendedNormal lecture attendance or credit received through an Adjustment.
Surprise Test / Class TestLecture outcomes associated with those assessment activities.
Teacher Absent / No Classes / AbsentThe corresponding scheduled-lecture outcomes.

An unmarked lecture can appear temporarily as In Progress from its scheduled start until its scheduled end. If it remains unmarked after the end time, it is treated as Absent for the relevant lecture analytics. In Progress is derived for display and is not saved as an attendance status.

Use the information/legend control in Lecture Wise Percentage to review the outcome categories. The same semester-scoped breakdown and percentage views are available across the isolated semester environments.

🏖️ 10. Holidays, Blank & Special Days

Holiday records a college holiday and requires the requested reason. Blank is an intentional unmarked/not-applicable day and is excluded from attendance and lecture graph calculations. Select the applicable Blank reason when prompted.

Protected Holidays and protected Blank dates are seeded by INVICTUS. They are shown as automatic records and cannot be changed through normal Submit/Edit Attendance controls.

Use the day-level attendance status for holidays and other non-class days rather than trying to mark individual lectures that did not take place.

📝 11. All Submitted Results

Open Semester → All Submitted Results to review and manage the active semester's submitted assessment results. Each semester has its own result records.

The result area contains separate sections for Subject, Surprise Tests, and Exam Results. Subject entries appear when that subject has completed submitted results; the displayed count indicates how many completed entries exist. Pending entries are available through the bell and remain subject to their existing completion and validation flows.

Open a result entry to view its report details. Existing editing and deletion controls remain available according to the result type and its rules. Exam Results that have not yet been completed can remain in Pending Results until their required details are submitted.

In Exam Results, press and hold a result card, then drag it to change its order within the selected exam type. Tap Save to keep the arrangement. Reset restores the default order for that selected exam type. The arrangement is independent for each semester and exam type and is included in data backups.

Lecture Breakdown and All Submitted Results are mutually exclusive parent cards. Opening either one closes the other. This behaviour applies across Semesters 1–6.

📈 12. Dashboard

Dashboard is a read-only progress overview. Its cards include:

Weekly TimetableView the weekly schedule for a semester.
Monthly - Lectures StatusReview lecture-level status for the selected month.
Monthly ReportReview day-level Present, Absent, and Holiday attendance for the selected month.
Semester - Lectures StatusReview lecture progress across a configured semester.
Semester ReportReview day-level attendance for a configured semester.

The two monthly cards each have their own temporary semester filter. Both start at All Semesters. Choose Semester 1–6 to limit that card's month navigation to the selected semester's configured date range. The two card filters are independent and do not change Settings → Set Semester.

Choosing All Semesters returns that card to the current calendar month. Collapsing a monthly card resets its filter to All Semesters and its month to the current month. Reopening it starts with those defaults.

Dashboard reports are date-driven: the report date determines which semester's isolated attendance data is read. The global Set Semester choice does not change the Dashboard reporting context. Current/future dates are handled according to the existing report timeline and date cutoffs.

🎓 13. Semester System

INVICTUS provides separate environments for Semesters 1–6. Each semester has its own configured working period, attendance and lecture records, subjects, timetable, breakdown, and assessment results.

All six semesters can be selected through Settings → Account → Set Semester. A semester is not hidden merely because its configured start date is in the future.

The selected semester is the active context for semester-scoped areas such as Daily Timetable, Lecture Breakdown, All Submitted Results, and Weekly Timetable. Switching semesters does not merge or share their underlying records.

The session's Set Semester choice remains active while moving between tabs. On a refresh or new app launch, INVICTUS selects the semester containing the current date according to the configured semester ranges.

👤 14. Profile & Account

Open Settings → Account to manage your profile and account:

Add Profile photo / Change Profile photoAdd a profile photo when none is saved, or change the existing photo. Tap the displayed photo to view it; deletion requires confirmation.
Add Name / Edit Name; Add Gmail / Edit GmailAdd a name or Gmail address when the field is empty, or edit the saved value. Gmail is optional for normal app use, but a valid Gmail address is required for the external Update and Assignment Collection actions.
Edit Joined dateChange the saved College Joining Date. The current date is prefilled; save a date from 08 August 2026 through today.
Set SemesterChoose the active semester for semester-scoped areas during the current app session.
Log outEnd the current account session. Profile information is retained or cleared according to Profile Saving.

⚙️ 15. Settings

Settings is organized into expandable category cards:

AccountProfile photo, name, Gmail, Edit Joined date, Set Semester, and Log out.
AppApp refresh/update controls and the Profile Saving preference.
NotificationsTurn the available attendance and lecture reminders on or off.
DataExport, import, or reset application data.
ZoomAdjust the overall app display size. INVICTUS text uses its normal 100% font-size baseline without a separate Font Size control.
SecurityManage the available Student ID and Password options.
AppearanceChoose Light or Dark Appearance.
Help / AboutOpen this User Guide or read information about INVICTUS.

Expanded Settings categories use the app's disclosure-card layout. Open a category to view its controls, then collapse it when finished.

🔔 16. Notification Centre

Tap the profile-card bell to open the in-app Notification Centre.

Submit Your AttendanceCan appear when today's attendance has not been submitted during the applicable submission window.
Mark Your Today LectureCan appear after an eligible timetable lecture ends while it still needs to be marked.

Use Settings → Notifications to control the available reminders. These notifications are displayed inside INVICTUS; they are not browser or Android system notifications.

The Semester results bell is separate: it opens Pending Results for the active semester.

💾 17. Backup, Profile Saving & Reset

Create Backup creates a backup of supported INVICTUS records. Import Backup restores data from a compatible INVICTUS backup. Keep a recent backup before making changes you may later want to reverse.

Settings → App → Profile Saving is ON by default. When enabled, the College Joining Date, profile photo, name, and Gmail are retained across logout and Reset All App Data. When disabled, those profile details are cleared by logout/reset. The Profile Saving preference itself is retained.

⚠️ Reset All App Data clears saved application records and resets most app preferences. It is not the same as changing your College Joining Date or using Set to Default in Schedule Exception. Check Profile Saving and export a backup before resetting.

The manually selected Set Semester value is a session choice. Refreshing or reopening INVICTUS selects the semester containing the current date from the configured ranges.

💡 18. Quick Daily Routine

1. Open Attendance and submit today's college-day status.

2. When unresolved dates are found, resolve Gate 1 and Gate 2 as appropriate, or use the corresponding Continue to Today's Attendance Anyway option for that submission attempt.

3. Open Semester → Daily Timetable and mark lectures after they become eligible.

4. Use Schedule Exception → Set Adjustment only when you attended a different subject during a scheduled period.

5. Use Schedule Exception → Set Replacement when the lecture occupying a slot actually changed.

6. Check the profile bell for attendance/lecture reminders and the Semester results bell for pending assessments.

7. Review Dashboard, Lecture Breakdown, Lecture Wise Percentage, and All Submitted Results to follow your progress.

✅ 19. You're Ready to Use INVICTUS

Attendance records your college day, Semester manages your timetable and assessments, Dashboard presents date-driven progress reports, and Settings controls your account and app preferences.

Remember the main workflow: daily attendance → resolve or temporarily bypass the applicable unresolved-attendance gates → mark completed lectures → use schedule exceptions only when needed → review results and progress.

To revisit any topic, open Settings → Help → User Guide and scroll to the relevant section.
